Student Copy Creator

Paste a teacher copy Google Doc link. You get a new student copy in the same folder. The teacher copy is not changed.

What it does: removes the cover pages, the "TEACHER COPY" label, and the "Answers in blue" and test-security notes. Blue answers become blank writing lines. The correct choice in a blue-marked list goes back to plain text. The "*" on optional questions is removed. Always skim the result before you share it.
