Student Copy Creator

Paste a teacher copy Google Doc link. You get a new student copy in the same folder. The teacher copy is not changed.

Before you start: resolve comments and make sure copy-editing feedback is in (step 1 of the how-to). The tool then removes the cover pages, blue answers and notes, optional (*) questions, "Notes to Teacher" boxes and highlights. It also swaps "Teacher Copy" for "Student Copy" in the header and the copyright line for the CC BY-NC-SA line in the footer. Comments are not copied.
